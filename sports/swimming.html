04 · Format & rules

How a match is won

50

metres, one length

  1. 01

    Heats and a final

    Swimmers race in heats; the fastest eight swim the final.

  2. 02

    Freestyle

    Any stroke is allowed; most swimmers choose front crawl.

  3. 03

    Medley relay

    Four swimmers race backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly and freestyle, in that order.

  4. 04

    Touch to finish

    The race ends when the swimmer touches the wall pad; times are read to the hundredth.

  5. 05

    One start

    A swimmer who leaves the block before the signal is disqualified.

GoldWinner of the final

Athletes

Enter through the athletes’ entrance of the Aquatic Stadium with your accreditation card; warm-up and call room are signposted from the door.

Officials and guests

Technical officials and VIP guests use the officials’ entrance; volunteers at the door direct you to your seats.

Spectators

Follow the signs from the main plaza of the complex. Seating is first come, first served for every session.
