02 · Format & rules

How a match is won

21

points to win a game

  1. 01

    Rally-point scoring

    Every rally scores a point, whoever served.

  2. 02

    Win by two

    A game goes to 21, but must be won by two clear points. At 29–29 the next point wins it, 30 caps the game.

  3. 03

    Best of three games

    The first player or pair to take two games wins the match.

  4. 04

    Intervals

    A 60-second break when the leader reaches 11, and up to two minutes between games. Ends change after each game, and at 11 in a deciding game.

  5. 05

    Knockout draw

    Rounds of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final for every event.
