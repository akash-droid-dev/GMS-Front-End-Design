04 · Format & rules

How a match is won

6

games to win a set

  1. 01

    Sets to six games

    A set goes to six games, won by two; at 6–6 a tie-break decides it.

  2. 02

    Best of three sets

    The first player or pair to win two sets wins the match.

  3. 03

    Scoring

    Points run 15, 30, 40 and game; at 40–40 a player needs two clear points.

  4. 04

    Mixed doubles

    One man and one woman per side, serving in turn.

  5. 05

    Knockout draw

    Knockout from the first round to the final, for every event.

Walking times inside the complex, from the organising team’s venue guide.

Athletes

Enter through the athletes’ entrance of the Tennis Courts with your accreditation card; warm-up and call room are signposted from the door.

Officials and guests

Technical officials and VIP guests use the officials’ entrance; volunteers at the door direct you to your seats.

Spectators

Follow the signs from the main plaza of the complex. Seating is first come, first served for every session.
