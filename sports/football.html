02 · Format & rules

How a match is won

90

minutes of play

  1. 01

    Two halves

    A match is two halves of 45 minutes; the team with more goals wins.

  2. 02

    Knockout ties

    A drawn knockout match goes to a penalty shoot-out.

  3. 03

    Five-a-side

    Smaller pitch, five players a side including the goalkeeper, rolling substitutions.

  4. 04

    Cards

    Two yellow cards in a match mean a red; a red card rules the player out of the next match.

  5. 05

    Groups, then knockouts

    Group games lead into semi-finals and the medal matches.

GoldWinner of the final

Athletes

Enter through the athletes’ entrance of the Outdoor Sports Ground with your accreditation card; warm-up and call room are signposted from the door.

Officials and guests

Technical officials and VIP guests use the officials’ entrance; volunteers at the door direct you to your seats.

Spectators

Follow the signs from the main plaza of the complex. Seating is first come, first served for every session.
