# Role photos: prompts for ChatGPT

Every role needs 3 photos of the **same person** in the **same spot**:

| File name | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `<role>-0.png` | Rest pose (standing still) |
| `<role>-1.png` | The action halfway through |
| `<role>-2.png` | The action at its peak |

**How to do it, per role (one ChatGPT chat per role works best):**

1. Start a new chat. Upload `athlete-0.png` from this folder and paste the **REST** prompt. This keeps every role in the same studio style.
2. Download the result and save it here as `<role>-0.png`.
3. In the **same chat**, upload that `<role>-0.png` and paste the **MID** prompt. Save it as `<role>-1.png`.
4. Upload `<role>-0.png` again and paste the **PEAK** prompt. Save it as `<role>-2.png`.
5. Ask for a **wide 16:9 landscape** image if ChatGPT gives you a square one.

Already done: `athlete-0/1/2.png` and `lo-0.png`. For Liaison Officer only steps 3 and 4 are left.

When a few are in, tell me and I'll process and wire them into the site. Any role that has no photos yet keeps its drawn figure until it does.

## Shared style (already included in every REST prompt)

> Photorealistic wide 16:9 landscape studio photograph, full body, side view facing right. The person stands on the left third of the frame and the right side is empty white space. Pure white seamless studio background and floor, soft even daylight from the front-right, a subtle soft contact shadow on the floor, natural skin texture, sharp focus, 85mm lens, editorial photography. Same framing and lighting as the uploaded reference photo. No text, no logos.

## Edit rule (already included in every MID and PEAK prompt)

> Same person, same outfit, same camera angle, framing, lens, lighting and white studio as the uploaded photo. Keep them standing in the same spot. Change only the pose: …
