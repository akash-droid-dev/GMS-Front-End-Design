# Role loop videos: prompts for Google Gemini (Veo)

Each of the 12 roles gets a **short seamless loop** of the same person from the photos, doing their job. The site plays it on hover (desktop) or when the role is open (phones).

## Settings for every clip

- **Image-to-video:** upload that role's rest photo (`<role>-0.png` from this folder) as the **starting frame**, so the person, outfit and studio match exactly.
- **16:9, 1080p, 6 seconds.**
- Save as `<role>-loop.mp4` (for example `staff-loop.mp4`) in this folder.

## Shared ending: paste this after every role's action line

> Photorealistic, one continuous shot, locked-off camera on a tripod (no camera movement, no zoom, no cuts), same framing, person, outfit and pure white seamless studio as the uploaded photo, the person stays standing in the same spot on the left third of the frame. Natural, realistic body motion at real-time speed (not slow motion), soft high-key lighting, sharp focus. The clip must loop seamlessly: the last frame returns to exactly the same pose as the first frame. No text, no logos, no watermark, no other people, no music.

## Tips

- **Camera moves:** add "the camera is completely static on a tripod for the entire clip."
- **Doesn't loop cleanly:** add "the first and last frames are identical."
- **Person drifts across the frame:** add "the person stays in the same spot the whole time."
- **Gemini adds a sparkle watermark:** fine, I remove it when I process the clips.

When the clips are in this folder, tell me. I'll compress them (about 0.5–1 MB each), loop them in the roles section and keep the photos as instant first frames.
